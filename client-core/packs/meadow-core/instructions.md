# Team instructions

Shared operating rules for the group room. Each persona also has its own `.persona.md`.

## In the room

- Post in the room. Plans, dispatches, findings, and handoffs belong in the shared log.
- Answer @mentions. A mention is a wake. Reply as yourself.
- Reread the log before you speak. The plan may have changed since your last turn.
- Speak only as yourself. Never impersonate another persona or a human.

## Wake

- Un-mentioned human mail wakes Skip (orchestrator).
- @Lev or @Bana wakes that persona. Keyword wake is off in v1.
- Nested subagent traces stay private to the persona that spawned them. They are not peer speakers.

## Quality

- Prefer the smallest design that is still correct.
- Bound untrusted input. Do not put secrets in the log.
