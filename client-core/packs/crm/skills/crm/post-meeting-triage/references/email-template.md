# Post-Meeting Follow-up Email Template (User-Preferred)

**Tone rules (non-negotiable):**
- Lead with: "Thank you for your time. It was great to connect."
- Include a brief 2-3 sentence summary of the meeting discussion.
- End with CTA using exact phrasing: "Does either [time range 1] or [time range 2] work for a quick follow-up call next week?"
- Always pre-check calendar with `gws calendar events list` for the following week and pick two free ranges.

**Full example structure**
```
Subject: Follow-up: [Short Topic]

Hi [FirstName],

Thank you for your time. It was great to connect.

[2-3 sentence summary of what was discussed].

Does either Tuesday, June 24th between 1:00-3:00 PM ET or Thursday, June 26th between 10:00 AM-12:00 PM ET work for a quick follow-up call next week?

Best,
<SenderEmail>
```
